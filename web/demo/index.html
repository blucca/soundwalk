English transcript

A wave. A bird nearby. A gap in the traffic. SoundWalk gives you one small reason to stop and listen.

Outdoors, you record five seconds. Here, I’m using the credited shoreline sample. The open YAMNet model runs in this browser and suggests water.

You choose what to follow. This invitation asks you to pick out one small splash within the larger wash of sound.

Then the page dims for sixty seconds. Read once, look up, and listen. This film skips ahead through the minute.

When you return, write one thing you noticed. Here is a scripted example. The card keeps the model’s suggestion, your choice, and your words.

Save an image, or keep your field notes on this device. After preparation, the model works offline. Try SoundWalk, and bring back a sound.
